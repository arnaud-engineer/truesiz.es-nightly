# GPT Device Data Detector 0.2: Distributed Source

All editable engine modules and declarations are in src/. LICENSE is MPL-2.0.
The catalogue is an explicit, editable declaration graph derived from inherited
data.js (also supplied). scripts/import-legacy.mjs and scripts/build-graph.mjs
are its preferred generators. The former requires TypeScript 6.0.2 on Node 22+.
Neither generator evaluates the inherited data.js.

The original source revision is e0daafc9100dc7ae8f1d469990b6e6962a0cac20:
https://github.com/arnaud-engineer/device-data-detector/blob/e0daafc9100dc7ae8f1d469990b6e6962a0cac20/data.js
The actual received source SHA-256 is recorded in every graph assertion.
Declarations and proposed edges remain legacy-unverified; aliases are empty.
Names or nominal diagonals cannot determine the active panel or CSS/cm.

assets/*.svg are Lucide 1.52.0 icons. assets/LUCIDE-LICENSE preserves the ISC and
applicable Feather MIT notices. No Renderer or its compiled bundle is included.
GUIDE.txt describes the public API, lifecycle, data semantics and limitations.
manifest.json lists exact content hashes, not a physical certification.

Maintained engine and guide: the owner's canonical GPT-device-data-detector
repository. Maintained Nightly application: truesiz.es-nightly. No stable-site
integration is authorized. No model, display label, UA or calibration is uploaded
by the engine. Static hosting still receives ordinary asset requests.
